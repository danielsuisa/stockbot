The window is the last trading day of the month plus the first three trading days of the next month. The book buys SPY at the close of the session before that last day and sells at the close of the third session of the next month. Every other session is cash at 0%. Prices are Yahoo Finance chart v8 adjusted closes. The commission uses the official close when it exists. In-sample exits fall in 2000 through 2014. Out-of-sample exits fall in 2015 through the last session.

This is the quick screen. Each window is a fresh $460, not a compounded path. The pre-tax annual rate is the mean out-of-sample window return times 12, minus the mean Fixed commission on two orders times 12. A year's net is the sum of that year's net window returns. A winning year is taxed at 25%. A losing year pays nothing and does not carry forward. The after-tax rate scales the pre-tax rate by the tax due on those year nets. Buy-and-hold SPY is taxed once, on the gain from the first out-of-sample entry close to the last session, after its own entry and exit. Close means the after-tax rate is within 1 percentage point of SPY's after-tax CAGR, or ahead of it. The compounded path runs only when the sample meets n and the screen is close.
