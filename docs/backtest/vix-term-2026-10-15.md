## After tax

Israeli capital-gains tax at 25%. Realized losses offset realized gains inside the same calendar year. A year that nets to a loss pays nothing and does not carry forward. There is no refund. The tax is subtracted from terminal equity and is not compounded inside the path. SGOV and BIL gains are taxed the same way. There is no exemption. Sharpe and maximum drawdown stay pre-tax.

The strategy's tax line adds one final mark: shares times the last close, minus the all-in cost basis, with no extra spread. That mark is not a trade in the Sharpe path. Buy-and-hold SPY is taxed once, on the gain from the starting $460 to its terminal equity.
