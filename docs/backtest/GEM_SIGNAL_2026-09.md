# GEM signal — month-end September 2026

The signal date is the last trading day of September 2026, Wednesday 2026-09-30. This is the original GEM rule: SPY's 12-month total return against BIL's; if SPY wins, the higher 12-month total return of SPY and VEU; otherwise AGG. A tie is not a win. There is no gap and no hysteresis. Prices are Yahoo Finance chart v8 adjusted closes, which include dividends. This note does not place or transmit an order, and it does not compute a return.

The 12-month total return is the adjusted close on 2026-09-30 divided by the adjusted close on 2026-09-30 one year earlier, 2025-09-30, minus one. Both dates are the last session of the month.
