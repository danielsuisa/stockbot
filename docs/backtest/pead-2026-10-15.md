## Cost model

A name priced at $5 or above keeps the earlier model: the full SIP quote during the 09:30 minute of the entry, scaled into split-adjusted dollars, added on top of the printed open and the printed exit. A missing quote on that name is the 1% round-trip haircut. IBKR Pro adds $0.0035 a share with a $0.35 minimum, both sides. IBKR Lite zeros that commission and keeps the same spread. The gate is the 20-session Pro book, and its mean net return still has to beat buy-and-hold SPY.

A name whose entry print is strictly under $5 uses the amended model. The entry fills at the ask and the exit at the bid when both SIP quotes exist. The entry quote is the 09:30 minute. The exit quote is the session close when the exit is a close. A stop has no minute on a daily bar, so a missing side drops both sides onto a percent of the print: the entry is raised by 1% and the exit is lowered by 1%. That is 1% on each side, not a 1% round trip. The 0.5% and 2% columns use the same quotes and change only that fallback. They do not flip the verdict.
