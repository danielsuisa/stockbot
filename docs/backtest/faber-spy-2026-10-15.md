# Faber 10-month filter on SPY — T10

Run date 2026-10-15. The last session is 2026-10-14. This is a paper test. It places no order.

At the close of each month's last trading day, the book holds the equity sleeve when SPY's adjusted close is strictly above the average of the last ten month-end adjusted closes, including that month. Otherwise the recorded book holds cash at 0%. A close equal to the average is cash. The parameter was published in 2007 and is not fitted. The whole window from the December 2006 signal through the last session is one out-of-sample period. The December 2006 close is the first order. It needs the ten month-ends ending that month. This is not the earlier SPYM whole-share Faber report on the GEM window.

The gate has two rows, and both sides of a row use the same share rule. Row (a) is fractional units. Row (b) is whole shares of a $460 account, and SPY exposure is SPYM because one SPY share costs more than $460. The verdict is row (b), and that row holds cash at 0% when it is out of the market. A BIL sleeve is reported on the same signals as information only. It pays a commission on every BIL entry and every BIL exit. Before BIL's first print (2007-05-30) the sleeve is cash. The sleeve does not add a trial. This re-run is a bug fix, so N stays 18.

Commission is IBKR Pro Fixed, $0.005 a share, minimum $1, maximum 1% of trade value, with no extra spread. A switch trades at the month-end close, and the next month's moves belong to the new holding. The last session liquidates so the exit cost is in the result. That sale is not counted as a switch. Row (a) marks the adjusted close. Row (b) marks the actual close, pays dividends as cash, and applies splits to shares already held. Sharpe is daily, the risk-free rate is zero, and the scale is the square root of 252. No tax is computed. Buy-and-hold pays one entry and one exit.

## Required sample

T10 has no in-sample period. The 10-month average was published in 2007 and is not fitted here. Required n is not computed. The sample-size gate does not apply. The verdict is `trend_verdict` for `faber-spy` in `docs/backtest/gates.json`, on row (b), cash at 0%. Both of these are required: CAGR no further below the benchmark than the gate's shortfall, and the paired block bootstrap at or above the gate's floor. `dsr_n_next` stays 18. This run does not call the trial counter.
