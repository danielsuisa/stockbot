# Faber 10-month filter on SPY — T10

Run date 2026-10-15. The last session is 2026-10-14. This is a paper test. It places no order.

At the close of each month's last trading day, the book holds SPY when that close is strictly above the average of the last ten month-end adjusted closes, including that month. Otherwise it holds cash at 0%. A close equal to the average is cash. The parameter was published in 2007 and is not fitted. The whole window from the December 2006 signal through the last session is one out-of-sample period. The December 2006 close is the first order. It needs the ten month-ends ending that month. This is not the earlier SPYM whole-share Faber report.

The account starts at $460 and may hold fractional shares. Commission is IBKR Pro Fixed, $0.005 a share, minimum $1, maximum 1% of trade value, with no extra spread. A switch trades at the month-end close, and the next month's moves belong to the new holding. The last session liquidates so the exit cost is in the result. That sale is not counted as a switch. Sharpe is daily, the risk-free rate is zero, and the scale is the square root of 252. Maximum drawdown and Sharpe use the pre-tax path. The 25% tax nets each calendar year's realized gains and is subtracted once from terminal equity. Buy-and-hold SPY pays one entry, one exit, and one tax at the end.

## Required sample

T10 has no in-sample period. The 10-month average was published in 2007 and is not fitted here. Required n is not computed. The sample-size gate does not apply. The verdict is the drawdown comparison: GO only when the pre-tax maximum drawdown is strictly smaller than SPY's. Return is reported and is not the gate.
